# references

What the seqno brand is built on. Bassim's saves come from his data folder on the Mac (`me.db`: X bookmarks and likes synced through Sep 23 2026, Raindrop through Sep 17 2026), read on Oct 8 2026. One line each on what was taken.

## The two sites in the brief

1. **Buzz**, https://buzz.xyz (captured in headless Chrome, Oct 8 2026). The finish and the hero's structure: one bold field color, an oversized grainy and blurred wordmark, a faint dot grid, small creatures that drift and flee the cursor, an 11px mono label, a two-line tagline, almost nothing else. Measured from the page: dot grid 1.2px dots at 16% ink every 37px; the wordmark is Cash Sans 700 at −0.04em through an SVG filter (blur 16, fractal-noise displacement 24, noise seed animated every 0.4 s); the label is Cash Sans Mono 11px at +0.14em; the tagline is 32/34. Taken: the field, the grain, the dot grid, the mono label and its size, the creatures. Changed so it's seqno's: the grain is still instead of buzzing; the blur means something (the wordmark sharpens as the outlines form); the grid is 24px because it is the outliner's row; the creatures are bullets that settle into outlines instead of bees; the color is warm apricot.
2. **Logseq's 2021 site**, https://web.archive.org/web/20210702034118/https://logseq.com/ (same page through Dec 2021). The page order: a small status chip ("BETA TESTING"), headline, paragraph and two buttons; "Your data is yours, forever!" with a real app screenshot; feature rows alternating text and screenshots; a closing call to action. Taken: that order, cut down, and the honest status chip, which became "Work in progress · Nº 0194". Not taken: the dark hero, testimonials, the dot-pattern decoration that meant nothing, and anything that pictures features the app doesn't have.

## His own projects

- **hex site** (`~/Developer/hex-site`, `~/Developer/hex-site-shots`). The craft bar: tokens in one place, light and dark as equals, real numbers in the hero (~2,100 lines), before/after shots. Not its look: no thick borders, offset shadows or sticker colors here.
- **commonplace brand** (`~/Developer/commonplace-site/brand`). The structure of this folder (BRAND.md sections, references.md, explorations argued in the specimen, `gen.mjs` and `render.mjs`). Not its look: no Plex, no red, no terminal.

## Outside references that fit

- **The numero sign** (№, nº). The abbreviation of *numero*, written as an n and a raised o over a line. It is literally "no." for number, which is what the "no" in seqno is.
- **Dot-grid notebooks** (the 5mm dot grid of bullet journals). A page of empty rows waiting for bullets; the hero is one of those pages in apricot.
- **seqno's own design spec** (`~/dev/seqno/docs/design-spec.md`). The bullet (6.4px, round), the 1px guide under a parent's bullet, and the row height come from the app, so the brand draws the same thing the app draws.

## What was deliberately not taken

- Logseq's logo, teal palettes, screenshots, illustrations or text. Logseq is AGPL and trademarked; seqno is named next to it only to say what it opens.
- Buzz's chartreuse, its bee, its animated (buzzing) noise, and Cash Sans.
- Graph-of-nodes "knowledge graph" visuals, glowing gradients, glassmorphism, 3D hero objects, ASCII wallpaper.
- "Coming soon" and waitlists. The work-in-progress state is a number on the page.
